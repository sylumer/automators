# Overview
Micro blogging is the frequent posting of short posts to a blog, often referred to as a micro blog for its use in such blogging practices. Social platforms such as [[Twitter|X/Twitter]] have often been referred to as microblogs, but their are dedicated platforms too such as [[micro.blog]], as well as people who simply use a typical blogging platform (e.g. [[WordPress]]) for hosting microblog content.
