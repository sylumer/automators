# Overview
Gamification is the process through which elements of games such as competitions, scores (and scoreboards), and awards are used to drive and encourage specific user activity. This is utilising human psychology and aims to trigger dopamine releases for small compound achievements that align to an app or platform's desired user behaviour - e.g. continued or increased use.

Gamification can be applied in both societally positive (e.g. educational), and negative (e.g. financial/time expenditure) ways, and while there will always be an aspect to driving buy-in/commitment to an app/platform, the additional benefits to the user should be assessed to determine whether it is societally a positive or negative use of gamification.
