# Overview
Micro.blog is a platform that blends independent blog hosting with a social media-style timeline, prioritising user ownership and a safe community experience over advertising and algorithms. Unlike traditional social networks that silo your content, Micro.blog encourages users to post content to their own personal website (which it can host for a fee or integrate via [[RSS]] from elsewhere) while still participating in a centralised, chronological timeline with replies and bookmarks. It is a deliberate effort to return control of content to the individual, promoting thoughtful conversation in an environment free from the pressures of follower counts and viral trends.

# Features
- **Content Ownership**: Encourages publishing to your own domain name where you maintain full control over your content.
- **Chronological Timeline**: The main timeline displays all posts in the order they were published, free from algorithmic interference or advertising insertions.
- **Integrated Hosting**: Offers simple, affordable blog hosting services for both short micro-posts and longer articles.
- **Fediverse Support**: Part of the fediverse and supports open web standards like [[ActivityPub]], [[Webmention]], and [[Micropub]], allowing interaction with other decentralised platforms - e.g. [[Mastodon]].
- **Human-Curated Discover Section**: A "Discover" section is curated by a community manager, prioritising quality content over algorithmic trends and actively discouraging a "viral" environment.
- **No Public Popularity Metrics**: Follower counts and public "like" counts are intentionally hidden to reduce social pressure and competition.
- **Cross-Posting Capabilities**: Automatically cross-post content from your personal blog to other networks like X ([[Twitter]]), [[Bluesky]], [[Mastodon]], and more.
- **Multimedia Support**: Supports various content formats, including text, photos, podcasts, videos, and links.
- **Markdown Editor**: A simple web interface and companion apps that support [[Markdown]] for easy content creation.
- **Community Guidelines**: A strong emphasis on a safe, friendly community with enforced community guidelines to prevent abuse and trolling. 

# Notes
- The platform was created by Manton Reece, who launched a successful Kickstarter campaign in 2017 to fund its development and his accompanying book, *Indie Microblogging*.
- Official support is only for the original (basic) [[Markdown]] specification.
