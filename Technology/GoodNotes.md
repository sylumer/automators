# Overview
GoodNotes is a digital paper and note-taking application that combines handwriting with digital tools. It is designed to help users capture ideas, organise documents, and work in a single, fluid environment. Originally an iPad exclusive, GoodNotes has expanded to include infinite canvas whiteboards and text documents (typing-focused) in addition to its core digital notebooks. It utilises leveraging advanced AI to make all content—whether handwritten, typed, or imported as a PDF—searchable and actionable.

# Features
- **Natural Handwritten Note-taking**: arguably provides a best-in-class, paper-like writing experience, with support for the [[Apple Pencil]], and includes palm rejection.
- **Handwriting Recognition**: Converts handwriting to text and makes all handwritten notes searchable within the app.
- **Multiple Document Types**: Create content on traditional page-based notebooks, whiteboards for brainstorming, or structured text documents. Also supports audio recording - useful for taking notes in lectures for example.
- **PDF Annotation and Import**: Easily import large PDFs, lecture slides, or documents and mark them up directly with pen, highlighter, and shape tools.
- **Organisation System**: Organise notes using nested folders, add bookmarks, and use the lasso tool to easily rearrange items within or across pages.
- **Collaboration Tools**: Share documents via links for real-time collaboration, commenting, and co-editing.
- **Customisable Stationery and Marketplace**: Access a variety of page templates, covers, digital stickers, and planners through the in-app Marketplace.
- **Cross-Platform Sync**: Sync notes and documents across Apple, Android, and Windows devices using [[iCloud]] or other cloud services like [[Google Drive]], [[OneDrive]] and [[Dropbox]].
