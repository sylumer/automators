# Overview
The Due app is a dedicated productivity utility for Apple devices that specialises in ensuring you never miss a critical task by using persistent (nagging) notifications that repeat until the reminder is marked complete or rescheduled. It has been around since 2010.

# Operating Systems
- macOS
- iOS
- iPadOS
- watchOS

# Features
- **Persistent Alerts (Auto Snooze)**: Continuously sends you periodic notifications for overdue reminders at customisable intervals (e.g., every minute, 5 minutes, 60 minutes) until you mark them as done.
- **Countdown Timers**: to the second countdown timers for repetitive tasks.
- **Quick Access Times**: Use 12 customisable buttons to instantly set a reminder to common future times (like "next 12 PM" or "next 10 PM") with a single tap.
- **Flexible Rescheduling**: Easily postpone reminders with customisable options right from the notification itself, or in bulk within the app.
- **Customisable Alert Sounds**: Assign distinctive alert sounds and lengths to different reminders, so louder, longer sounds can be used for the most important tasks.
- **Critical Alerts Support**: (On iOS) allows you to set certain alerts to bypass Do Not Disturb or silent mode, ensuring you hear them no matter what.

# Notes
- macOS and i\*OS apps are purchased separately, but also available via [[SetApp]].
- Reminders can be synchronised via [[iCloud]].
- Supports [[x-callback-url]] and [[Shortcuts]] for automation.
