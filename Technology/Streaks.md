# Overview
Streaks is a task app that helps you to develop daily habits by tracking and encouraging you to maintain an unbroken track record.

# Operating Systems
- iOS
- iPadOS
- watchOS
- macOS
- Vision Pro

# Notes
- Streaks is a Crunchy Bagel product.
- Automation is possible through [[Shortcuts]].
- Ref.: [Don't Break the Chain Productivity Method by Jerry Seinfield](https://www.asianefficiency.com/productivity/dont-break-chain/)
