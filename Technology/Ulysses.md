# Overview
Ulysses is a stylish and focused app for writing, available on a range of Apple devices. The core design is based on their own flavour of [[Markdown]] and is designed to help users concentrate on content and structure rather than formatting and layout. Documents are organised in a unified library, which can be broken down into "sheets" (individual text units) and "groups" (folders). This structure makes it useful for managing everything from short notes to large, complex projects like novels or dissertations, allowing users to easily rearrange sections via drag and drop and leaving final presentation for the export stage.

# Features

- **Minimal Interface**: A clean, editor with a full-screen mode to help maintain focus on writing.
- **Markdown-Based**: Uses [[Markdown]], but its own variation which can be problematic if moving content outside of Ulysses.
- **Integrated Document Management**: A single, powerful library system for organising all writing projects, notes, and research materials, with flexible hierarchy and keyword tagging.
- **iCloud Sync**
- **Writing Goals and Statistics**: Provides customisable metrics and progress goals (e.g., word count, reading time, character count) to track progress and help build writing habits.
- **Built-in Grammar and Style Check**: Features an advanced proofreader and editing assistant with suggestions for capitalisation, punctuation, style, and redundancy in over 20 languages.
- **Flexible Export Options**: Easily convert and export final work into various formats, including PDF, DOCX (Word), ePub (ebook), HTML, and plain text.
- **Direct Publishing**: Publish blog posts and articles directly to platforms like [[WordPress]], [[Medium]], [[Ghost]], and [[micro.blog]], complete with images and tags.
- **Customisation**: Offers a wide range of customisable themes, fonts, and editor settings to personalise the writing environment.
