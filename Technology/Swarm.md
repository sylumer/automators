# Overview
Swarm is an app by [[Foursquare]] that allows users to check-in at specific geographic locations. This enables users to build up a map of their check-in locations, track Swarm check-ins from friends, discover new locations tracked by others, and gain in-app awards through a [[Gamification]] process.
