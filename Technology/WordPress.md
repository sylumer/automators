# Overview
WordPress is a free, open-source content management system ([[Content Management System|CMS]]) that powers over 40% of all websites on the Internet, evolving from its origins as a simple blogging platform into a versatile website builder. It provides a user-friendly dashboard and a modular system of themes and plugins that allows individuals and businesses to create, manage, and customise virtually any type of online presence - from personal blogs and portfolios to complex e-commerce stores and corporate websites.

# Features

- **Open-Source Software**: The core software is free to use, modify, and distribute under the GPL license, promoting an active, community-driven development ecosystem.
- **User-Friendly Interface**: A simple dashboard and block-based editor (Gutenberg) make content creation, editing, and management straightforward for users of all skill levels.
- **Custom Themes**: Thousands of free and premium themes are available to instantly change the entire visual design and layout of a WordPress site without altering the underlying content.
- **Plugins**: A vast directory of plugins (> 60K) allows most functionality (e.g. e-commerce, contact forms, security enhancements, Search Engine Optimisation (SEO) tools) to be added with minimal effort.
- **SEO-Friendly Structure**: The platform is optimised for search engines right out of the box, with clean permalink structures and support for popular SEO plugins that help improve visibility and rankings.
- **Media Management**: An integrated media library makes it easy to upload, organise, and even perform basic edits (crop, resize) to images, audio, and video files.
- **User Management**: The platform supports different user roles and permissions, making it easier for teams to collaborate effectively.
- **Built-in Comments**: Includes integrated tools for managing and moderating comments and thus fostering community engagement around content.
- **Content Publishing Tools**: Features like drafts, post revisions, content scheduling, and password protection offer granular control over when and how content is published.
- **Mobile-Ready & Responsive**: The core software and most modern themes are designed to be mobile-friendly, ensuring websites look and function well on any device.
- **Community Support**: Due to its proliferation, there is a large global community providing extensive support through forums, tutorials, documentation, and local events (WordCamps).

# Notes
- Due to its penetration in the marketplace, WordPress is a large target for hackers. For a long time, WordPress suffered from a constant barrage of security updates, flawed plugins and the like. This has improved over the years, but security maintenance of your own WordPress instance is not necessarily a fair-weather exercise and trivial in nature. You need to be proactive and vigilant if you are going to host your own WordPress site.
- The WordPress software and its core trademarks are managed by the non-profit *WordPress Foundation*, established by Automattic's founder, Matt Mullenweg, to ensure the platform remains open-source and free, with Automattic holding licenses to use the brand for commercial products.
