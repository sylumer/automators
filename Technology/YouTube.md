# Overview
YouTube launched in 2005 and is a video sharing platform acquired by [[Google]] in 2006. Users can upload videos to share and these can be monetised through YouTube's creator revenue programme. Videos cover practically every topic imaginable and it is a veritable giant in online entertainment.

# Features
- Video hosting and streaming.
- Video transcription.
- Video editing (basic).
- Live streaming.
- Advertising and monetisation, plus premium subscription.
