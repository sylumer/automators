# Overview
Droplr is a versatile, cloud-based tool for file sharing. It can operates as a seamless background utility on desktop operating systems such as Mac and Windows, mobile apps, as a browser extension in modern desktop browsers, and via a web interface. 

# Features

- **Screen Capture & Annotation**: The apps allow quick capture of a selected area, full screen, or a specific window, markup with arrows, shapes, text, or a blur tool, and online sharing in one seamless workflow
- **Screen Recording**: Record your screen as an HD video or an animated GIF, with optional audio and webcam input, which is ideal for tutorials or bug reports.
- **Large File Transfer**: Easily upload files up to 10 GB via drag-and-drop functionality, which are then stored in the cloud and accessible via a link.
- **Automatic Link Shortening**: Every uploaded file or capture automatically generates a short link (e.g., `https://d.pr/123456`) that is immediately copied to your clipboard, ready for sharing.
- **Cloud Storage & Organisation**: All "drops" (shared items) are stored in your personal cloud workspace where they can be organised using boards (folders) and tags, and can be accessed from any device.
- **Security & Privacy Controls**: Offers options for password protection, setting files to "self-destruct" after a specific time, and team-only viewing for sensitive information.
- **Integrations**: Connects with popular collaboration and design tools such as [[Slack]], [[Trello]], [[Jira]], [[Microsoft Teams]], [[Gmail]], and [[Photoshop]] to streamline workflows.
- **Custom Branding**: Enterprise and team users can personalise shared drops with a custom domain and company logo for a professional appearance.
- **Analytics**: Provides basic viewer analytics to track how many people have seen or downloaded your shared files.
