# Overview
X, the platform formerly known as Twitter, is a global social networking service. Originally a social [[micro-blogging|microblogging]] platform, the platform is slowly being directed towards being an "everything app" that integrates audio, video, messaging, and soon, payment and banking capabilities (like [[WeChat]]. It allows users to share short "posts" (formerly "tweets") and connect with public figures, brands, and communities worldwide.

# Features
- **Posts**: Users can broadcast short messages of up to 280 characters, along with images, videos, and links (longer posts are available with a X Premium subscription).
- **Hashtags and Trending Topics**: Hashtags (`#`) are used to categorise content and join global conversations, with the "Explore" tab highlighting popular and emerging discussions.
- **Direct Messaging and Calling**: The app features private messaging (including encrypted chats) and supports voice and video calls between users.
- **Communities and Spaces**: Users can join interest-based groups for focused discussions or host/listen to live audio conversations in "Spaces".
- **Content Monetisation**: Creators can earn revenue through ad sharing, paid subscriptions, and tips.
- **AI Integration**: Features integration with xAI's Grok chatbot for conversational AI assistance.
- **Account Verification**: The legacy blue checkmark system has been replaced with a subscription model offering blue, gold (for businesses), or grey (for government) checkmarks.

# Notes
- Twitter was launched in 2006.
- Twitter was originally called "twttr".
- Elon Musk officially acquired Twitter for 44 billion USD on 28 October 2022, closing tout he deal just before a court-ordered deadline, after a convolutes process that began with his initial April offer, and included legal battles and attempts to back out of the deal.
- Musk is openly attempting to convert the platform into an "everything app", and has renamed it to "X" since the acquisition.
- Changes to operation (internal management and external moderation) continue to court controversy.
- Plans are underway to introduce "X Money Accounts" to enable peer-to-peer payments and other financial transactions within the app.
