# Overview
Scanbot is a mobile app that utilises a device camera to take a picture and converts it into a scanned document format. The app includes scanning assistance functionality such as deskewing, edge tracking & auto-crop, image filters and multiple export options.

# Operating Systems
- iOS

# Notes
- Originally known as Scanbot, it is now "Scanbot SDK" and "Scanbot SDK: Document Scanning".
- Acquired by Apryse, Scanbot has been rolled into an enterprise platform, and the standalone consumer functionality has unfortunately suffered as a result in comparison to other consumer focused scanning applications.
