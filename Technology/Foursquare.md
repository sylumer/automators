# Overview
Foursquare was an online platform developed circa 2008, launching in 2009, and was designed to allow users to check in at locations such as restaurants, businesses, and points of interest. Users could mark their visits on a map, share tips, and explore nearby options. A sort of social geo-tagging and check-in game.

FourSquare has pivoted to a broader geo-spatial computing services platform to provide location technology and data at scale. It continues to provide a consumer facing social check-in service via its [[Swarm]] app.
